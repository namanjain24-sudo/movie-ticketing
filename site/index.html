Browse

See what’s actually playing, with real certification, runtime, and language. No trailers, because none exist in this system.

Pick seats

Choose by price tier first, position second, same as the counter.

Hold

Seats lock for five minutes the moment you choose them. Nobody else can take them while you decide.

Pay

Pay against the clock. If the hold expires first, you were never charged.

Ticket

A ticket that was never oversold, waiting at the gate.

No invented data

Availability is a band, not a countdown

Plenty, filling, almost full, or sold out. Never a number that was only true one request ago.

Ratings are computed live

Every rating comes from real reviews, one per person, editable and never stackable. Nothing is fabricated to look fuller than it is.

Time comes from the server

Every countdown is anchored to server time, never the device clock, so a hold means exactly what it says.

Watching a seat map hold under five thousand people at once is more convincing than reading about it.
